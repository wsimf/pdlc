## Report style

Write the report the way you would tell a teammate what you did.

- Name the real symbol, path, command, and number. A sentence that could stand
  unchanged in another task's report says nothing about this one.
- Evidence is a pointer: a commit SHA, a `file:line`, or a command and its
  outcome. Never a paragraph.
- One thought per sentence, in plain words, naming who does what.
- Keep the fields above as short as the format shows them. Do not expand them
  into paragraphs, and do not compress sentences into arrows and abbreviations.
- Say plainly what you could not determine, and why.
